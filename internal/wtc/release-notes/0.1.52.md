# wtc 0.1.52

Services and tunnels that stop answering, or whose tunnel provider isn't
signed in, now say so instead of showing ready. On an OpenClaw gateway, a
repository with nothing on its branch yet shows its base, as in a local
collection.

## Services and tunnels

- **A service or tunnel that stops answering is no longer shown as ready.**
  Ready only meant that a startup probe passed. `wtc svc`, `wtc status` and
  the OpenClaw card now also look at each ready task's address as they
  report. One that gives no answer, or a 5xx such as a tunnel's 502 while the
  service behind it restarts, shows `not answering now` with what came back
  ([#156](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/wtc-cli/issues/156)).
- **A tunnel provider that isn't signed in is named.** A Tailscale tunnel
  whose client needs a login, is stopped or has an expired key, and a
  devtunnel whose login expired, show how to sign in again, and `wtc svc
  tunnel` refuses to open one until then
  ([#156](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/wtc-cli/issues/156)).

## OpenClaw: the card

- **A repository with nothing on its branch shows its base.** A gateway
  worktree is on its own `openclaw/<name>` branch from the start. Until
  something is on it (a commit, a change, a push or a pull request), the card
  shows its base (`main`) instead, as a detached worktree is shown in a local
  collection. The branch's name is in the tooltip
  ([#168](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/wtc-cli/issues/168)).
- **Catching up a branch with nothing on it says so.** When a gateway
  worktree's branch has no commits of its own, `wtc catch-up` fast-forwards
  it, and now reports it at `origin/<base>` again with nothing to publish,
  instead of "merged origin/main into openclaw/<name>; publish to send the
  merge" ([#168](https://github.com/lcorneliussen/wtc-cli/issues/168)).

## Upgrade

- **Install the new `wtc`.** On OpenClaw gateways, also run `wtc openclaw
  install` and reload open Control UI tabs.
- **Status looks at addresses now:** each pass sends one request, with a
  2-second limit, to every ready service and tunnel address, all at once, and
  asks Tailscale and devtunnel once whether they are signed in.
